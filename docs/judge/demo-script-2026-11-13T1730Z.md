# Outcome AI: demo scenes and the 1-minute video (v2.1.0: designs 37-40, updated from the 2026-11-13T0530Z script)

**Pay for verified outcomes, not tokens. Private by design, proven on NEAR. If privacy isn't proven, you get your money back.**

Everything below runs on the desk, tab 03 at http://127.0.0.1:8730, NEAR testnet. Datasets are synthetic: `smb-erp-fin`
(ERPNext export, "Orion") and `smb-fin-1` (bank statement, invoices, bills).
New since the 0530Z script: the buyer pays BEFORE any work (upfront flow, design-37); the result is delivered only after
ca-attest has verified every NEAR AI call of the job (design-39); a job that cannot be delivered is refunded on NEAR by
ChainAim's signer with a signed refund record (design-37); the receipt carries the money trail and the attestation
bundle (design-40); the signed quote carries the job's limits, including the attestation rule (design-38/39).

Before recording (once): the 0530Z steps (`-SignerKeys`, `-MandateKey`), the MANUAL_SETUP.md 2026-11-13 "Upfront pay"

## Scene 2: privacy not proven, money back (about 15 s on screen)

Wording rules for anything said or shown: say "unmasked only on your own machine" (the gateway runs in Docker on the PC,
127.0.0.1 only; a hosted gateway in a TEE is roadmap); say "every NEAR AI call attested" or "this job's model call ran
attested", not "every AI call attested" in general (a Bedrock call is not attested; Scene 3 is the policy refusing exactly
that). On one PC, say "a separate signer service" or "separate runtime", not "a separate server" (unless docs/deploy/
signer-cloud.md was followed and -SignerUrl is the cloud URL). Never say "HIPAA compliant". Lead with what stays private
(the content); do not call the payment private: payer, amount and time are public on NEAR, and Scene 1 shows it. Say
"refunded on NEAR", not "refunded instantly": a refund is one NEAR transaction that takes seconds to be final.
