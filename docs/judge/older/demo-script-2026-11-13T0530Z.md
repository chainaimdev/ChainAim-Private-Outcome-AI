# Outcome AI: demo scenes and the 1-minute video (privateAI-design-36, updated from the 2026-11-13T0230Z script)

**Pay for verified outcomes, not tokens. Private by design, proven on NEAR.**

Everything below runs on the desk (`.\test-console\desk.ps1 -All`), tab 03 at http://127.0.0.1:8730, NEAR testnet.
Datasets are synthetic: `smb-erp-fin` (ERPNext export, "Orion") and `smb-fin-1` (bank statement, invoices, bills).
New since the 0230Z script: ChainAim's signer runs as its own service (`ca-control-signer`, the control plane), and
every paid job is a two-sided AP2 deal: ChainAim signs the price, the tenant's agent signs its permission.

Before recording (once): `.\test-console\desk.ps1 -SignerKeys`, the MANUAL_SETUP.md 2026-11-13 "Signer split" steps,
`.\test-console\desk.ps1 -MandateKey -MandateTenant t02`, then `.\test-console\desk.ps1 -All -AllSkipTests`.

## Scene 1: a paid outcome, two signatures (about 40 s on screen)

Tab 03, dataset **Small Biz Financials - 2 (ERPNext)**, template **Credit limit exposure**, "Who pays?" **AI agent**,
tenant **t02**, mode **Estimate first**. Press Ask.

| Step on screen | What it proves | Blocker answered |
|---|---|---|
| "Quoted 0.02 USDC", then "Quote signed by ChainAim ✓" | a firm price from the files' sizes before any work, computed and AP2-signed by ChainAim's signer | unpredictable cost; pay for effort |
| "Spending policy: pay ✓ · quote still valid ✓ · attested route ✓ · ..." | the agent buys only inside its tenant's limits | agents can't buy safely |
| "Checkout mandate signed ✓ · tenant key ... · permits exactly this signed quote, for this payment only" | the buyer side of AP2: the tenant's own key signs its permission for this one quote and payment | two-sided deal |
| Settled on NEAR · transaction ↗ | USDC paid by the agent's NEAR account (relayer pays the gas), settled only after ChainAim's signer verified the mandate and the job ran | |
| Answer + "Every number in this answer was checked against the computed result" | numbers come from an exact query; the model only words them | hallucinated numbers |
| AI proof: ✓ 1 of 1 NEAR AI call verified | the model ran in an attested enclave, on de-identified text | "trust me" infrastructure; data leaves your control |
| "Mandate verified by ChainAim's signer ✓ (before the job ran; recorded in the receipt)" | the signer checked the agent's permission before any work; the receipt records it | proof, not promises |
| Signed receipt ✓ · quote ... · mandate by tenant t02 ... · verified by Agent t02 ✓ | one Ed25519 receipt binds the quote, the mandate and the NEAR payment, as hashes | |
| What the chain shows | payer, amount, time are public; the question, data, query, answer and route are not | stated limit |

## Scene 2: the agent refuses (about 10 s on screen)

Unchanged from the 0230Z script: the same agent, a free question, and a query model that NEAR AI does not serve
(Bedrock): the quote's route is not attested, so t02's policy ("attested NEAR AI route only") refuses it. Nothing is
signed (no mandate, no payment), nothing is paid, nothing runs.

```powershell
.\test-console\desk.ps1 -AgentAsk -Tenant t02 -Dataset smb-fin-1 -AgentQuestion "Which customers owe us the most, and how many days overdue is each?" -AgentQueryModel bedrock/nemotron-nano-3-30b
```

Expected: `Spending policy: refused · quote still valid OK · attested route NO · ...`, then `Not paid: refused by
t02's spending policy: ...; nothing was signed or paid`, `FAIL not paid (refused)`. Exit 1. No transaction, and no
"Checkout mandate signed" line (the mandate is signed only after the policy says pay).

Wording rules for anything said or shown: say "unmasked only on your own machine" (the gateway runs in Docker on the PC,
127.0.0.1 only; a hosted gateway in a TEE is roadmap); say "every NEAR AI call attested" or "this job's model call ran
attested", not "every AI call attested" in general (a Bedrock call is not attested; Scene 2 is the policy refusing exactly

(the content); do not call the payment private: payer, amount and time are public on NEAR, and Scene 1 shows it.
