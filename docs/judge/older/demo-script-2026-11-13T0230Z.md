# Outcome AI: demo scenes and the 1-minute video (privateAI-design-35 ARCH-653)

**Pay for verified outcomes, not tokens. Private by design, proven on NEAR.**

Everything below runs on the desk (`.\test-console\desk.ps1 -All`), tab 03 at http://127.0.0.1:8730, NEAR testnet.
Datasets are synthetic: `smb-erp-fin` (ERPNext export, "Orion") and `smb-fin-1` (bank statement, invoices, bills).

## Scene 1: a paid outcome (about 40 s on screen)

Tab 03, dataset **Small Biz Financials - 2 (ERPNext)**, template **Credit limit exposure**, "Who pays?" **AI agent**,
tenant **t02**, mode **Estimate first**. Press Ask.

| Step on screen | What it proves | Blocker answered |
|---|---|---|
| "Quoted 0.02 USDC", then "Quote signed by ChainAim ✓" | a firm price from the files' sizes before any work; ChainAim's AP2-signed checkout | unpredictable cost; pay for effort |
| "Spending policy: pay ✓ · quote still valid ✓ · attested route ✓ · at most … a job ✓ …" | the agent buys only inside its tenant's mandate | agents can't buy safely |
| Settled on NEAR · transaction ↗ | USDC paid by the agent's NEAR account (relayer pays the gas) | |
| Answer + "Every number in this answer was checked against the computed result" | numbers come from an exact query; the model only words them | hallucinated numbers |
| AI proof: ✓ 1 of 1 NEAR AI call verified | the model ran in an attested enclave, on de-identified text | "trust me" infrastructure; data leaves your control |
| Signed receipt ✓ · verified by Agent t02 ✓ | Ed25519 receipt: hashes of files, query, result, model call; the settlement | proof, not promises |
| What the chain shows | payer, amount, time are public; the question, data, query, answer and route are not | stated limit |

## Scene 2: the agent refuses (about 10 s on screen)

The same agent, a free question, and a query model that NEAR AI does not serve (Bedrock): the quote's route is not
attested, so a tenant whose policy says "attested NEAR AI route only" refuses it. Nothing is signed, nothing is paid,
nothing runs (estimate first: no work happens before payment).

Before recording, read tab 03's policy line for t02 (under "Who pays?"): it must end with "· attested NEAR AI route
only". If it does not, use the tenant whose line does.

```powershell
.\test-console\desk.ps1 -AgentAsk -Tenant t02 -Dataset smb-fin-1 -AgentQuestion "Which customers owe us the most, and how many days overdue is each?" -AgentQueryModel bedrock/nemotron-nano-3-30b
```

Expected (run live 2026-11-13: quoted 0.03, under every price limit): the agent's step reads
`Spending policy: refused · quote still valid OK · attested route NO · …` (the page shows ✓/✗ for OK/NO), then
`Not paid: refused by t02's spending policy: the policy requires the attested route and none was quoted; nothing was
signed or paid` and `FAIL    not paid (refused): see the last step`. Exit 1. No transaction.

Why a free question: a template's exact query calls no query model, so its route is the answer model alone (attested).
Packages/agent-policy `decide()` is the only thing that makes this decision; its tests cover each refusal
(expired, attested route, above maxPerJob, daily cap).

Wording rules for anything said or shown: say "unmasked only on your own machine" (the gateway runs in Docker on the PC,
127.0.0.1 only; a hosted gateway in a TEE is roadmap); say "every NEAR AI call attested" or "this job's model call ran attested",
not "every AI call attested" in general (a Bedrock call is not attested; Scene 2 is the policy refusing exactly that).
Never say "HIPAA compliant". Lead with what stays private (the content); do not call the payment private: payer, amount and time are public on NEAR, and Scene 1 shows it.
