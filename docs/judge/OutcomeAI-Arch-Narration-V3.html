ChainAim Outcome AI

Pay for verified outcomes, not tokens.Private by design.Proven on NEAR.

Regulated businesses need intelligence from large volumes of sensitive data, but frontier AI asks them to trust it with their data, their numbers and their budget. Outcome AI replaces that trust with proof. Your files and their rows stay on your PC; only masked text goes to an attested NEAR AI enclave; the agent pays on NEAR before the work, and gets its money back automatically if privacy is not proven. Press Play to hear a real run, step by step (NEAR testnet, 14 Nov 2026): run A in 10 steps (tenant t02, with one counter-offer, job 0ed4fe96…), run B (refund) in 4.

Narration uses your browser’s built-in voices. The smoothest are the “Natural” voices in Microsoft Edge on Windows (Indian English: Neerja, Prabhat; US English: Aria, Guy). Chrome offers Google voices.

Tenant's private runtime: the tenant's machine runs its own AI agent, its files and the ChainAim data plane (console, analyst, gateway with Presidio, ca-attest and the paywall), all in Docker on 127.0.0.1. ChainAim signer and control plane: for the hackathon a separate container (its own Docker project and keys) on the same tenant machine, so the separation is by design, not by hardware. Next: ChainAim hosts the signer and control plane (cloud, then a TEE), and the paywall with its relayer key moves there too.

Rules for judges

  1. No keys, no account, no install: Node.js 22.22+ and the repository.
  2. Run verify-receipt on docs/judge/sample-receipt-2026-11-14T0729Z.json: expect four OK lines (receipt, money trail, attestation, egress) and "counter of".
  3. Run observe-receipt: the same payment, read from NEAR.
  4. The Docker stack is optional; the video shows it running.

Built to extend

Modular by design: the data plane, ChainAim's control plane and settlement are separate services with signed, allow-listed interfaces. After the hackathon they move into dedicated cloud services, then into TEE workloads (NEAR AI / Shade Agents or Phala Cloud), with full ZK-PRET integration for verifiable trade-finance outcomes.
