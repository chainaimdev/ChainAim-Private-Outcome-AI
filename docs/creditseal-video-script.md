# ChainAim CreditSeal: 2-minute video script

**Tagline:** Private books, private models, public proof.

**Audience:** XRPL Lending & Borrowing judges, lenders/vault operators, banks and supervisors.

**Story in one line:** XRPL vaults now lend *without collateral* (XLS-66) and leave underwriting off-chain on purpose.
So the real question for savers is *should this loan be made at all?* CreditSeal answers it privately and puts the
proof inside the loan.

**Format:** 3 short slides (presentation) + one screen recording of the **CreditSeal Desk** (`http://127.0.0.1:8750`),
cut together. Voiceover is about 300 words: about 2:00 at 150 words a minute. Every number spoken below comes from
the Devnet runs recorded in `README.md` and `BUILD_LOG.md`.

## What makes it unique (use as on-screen callouts or a pinned comment)

1. **Fills the gap XLS-66 leaves on purpose:** underwriting for uncollateralised vault loans.
2. **It says no:** declines the stressed borrower, approves the healthy one, on the lender's own policy.
3. **Private by construction:** books stay on the borrower's PC; the pack carries IDs, never names.
4. **Computed, not generated:** XLS-66 formulas from the spec; AI only explains, and unverified numbers are flagged.
5. **Proof on-chain:** `sha256(pack)` in `LoanSet.Data`; a forged loan is refused before signing.
6. **Real identity:** live GLEIF LEI check (ISO 17442).
7. **Bank-readable:** ACTUS ANN contract reconciles with the XRPL loan to the cent.
8. **No failed transactions:** pre-flight checks the spec's failure conditions and names the clause.
9. **Savers see the downside:** depositor view of loss if a loan defaulted now.

## Before you record

- **Pre-run the slow parts.** Start the Desk with `demo\start-demo.ps1 -FreshVault`, then run step 1 (Kestrel's pilot
  loan) first so the impair/default timings have passed by the time you reach Scene 5. Steps 2, 3, 9, 10 and 15
  (the CFO's private question and the three on-chain fee payments) are skipped in the 2-minute cut; record them
  for a longer version or a B-roll of the ChainAim wallet collecting fees.
- **Say "Devnet" and "test dollar".** It runs on XRPL Devnet with a self-issued TUSD, not RLUSD and not MainNet.
- **Jupiter's identity is real; the financials are synthetic.** If the GLEIF line is on screen, keep the Desk's own
  note "Identity: real public GLEIF record. Financials: synthetic demo books." visible or add it as a lower-third.
- **Check the spoken numbers against your run.** 27k/month, 90 %, 154.6k, 2.38×, "six of six" and "about five
  percent" (10 % cover × 50 % = 5 %) come from the recorded runs; if your run prints different figures, change the
  voiceover, not the screen.
- **"ChainAim never signs"** is true of the code: Maya signs and Harbor co-signs; ChainAim's wallet only receives fees.
- **In this demo both sides run on one machine.** Don't claim the lender's model runs on a separate server; say
  "Harbor's private model" as the Desk does.
- **Timing tip:** if you run long, drop Scene 6 to one sentence or merge it into the close.
